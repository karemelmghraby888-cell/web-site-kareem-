First-year project

## SignBridge

### Sign language in, speech out. Speech in, text out.

SignBridge is an AI-powered portable embedded system that translates Arabic sign language into real-time spoken words using MediaPipe and low-latency audio caching. At the same time, a background microphone captures spoken dialogue and shows it as clean text on a touchscreen, so both people can talk without an interpreter.
